FLORAL & EVENT STYLING · ONE-PAGE REFERENCE
PRIMARY LOCKUP
Use the primary lockup wherever there is room for it. Minimum width 1.4 in (130 px on screen) — below that, switch to the wordmark or monogram. Keep clear space on all sides equal to the height of the capital E.
